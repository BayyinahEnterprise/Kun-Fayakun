## Description
Kun Fayakun ("Be, and it is") is a unified Python instrument suite for structural honesty verification, cryptographic integrity, and safety-critical dispatch. It brings six instruments under one codebase: FATIMA (154-layer cryptographic file integrity encoder, v10.8.0), Bayyinah (file and artifact integrity scanner), Furqan (structural honesty linter with Go, ONNX, and Python adapters), HABL (append-only binding ledger with SHA-256 hash chain), EESS (safety-critical dispatch engine with deterministic safety-state reduction, v0.3.24, 330 tests, 22 independent adversarial audit rounds, 21 defect families catalogued, 59 frozen invariants), and Shahid (weight-holder transparency reports). The architectural principles are grounded in five axioms of Structural Honesty Verification (SHV) derived from the Qur'an's own structural properties: append-only provenance (A1), surface-substrate consistency (A2), caller-side exhaustiveness (A3), projection consistency (A4), and evidence independence (A5). The repository includes three foundational documents: Kun Fayakun: The Story of Our Reality (914 pages, the theological and philosophical foundation), Al-Furqan: The Criterion, Divine Protection Edition (1,584 pages, the complete H-restored Qur'anic corpus), and the Bayyinah Collected Works research corpus (566,000+ lines, 91 works). Part of the Bayyinah Structural Honesty Research Program.
